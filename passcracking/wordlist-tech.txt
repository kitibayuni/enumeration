PASSWORD GENERATION
Find words relevant to the target's life:
- job names
- job roles
- birthday (if August 5, 1998 -> 0805, 1998, 08, 0508 0898)
- pet names
- family member names
- where they live
- hobbies

TURN THIS INTO A BASELIST.TXT

use the script to leetify the base list (mangler.py)
ex. python3 mangler.py baselist.txt > mangled.txt



AWK-METHOD for wordpairs:
awk 'NR==FNR{w[++n]=$0; next} {for(i=1;i<=n;i++) print $0 w[i]}' mangled.txt mangled.txt > wordpairs.txt


FILTER WORDPAIRS for specific length:
awk 'length($0) >= 12 && length($0) <= 24' wordpairs.txt > filtered.txt
