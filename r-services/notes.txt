a suite of services used to enable remote access or issue commands; developed by the computer systems research group

R-services span across the ports 512, 513, and 514 by default

They are a suite of programs known as r-commands, like:
rcp (remote copy)
rexec (remote execution)
rlogin (remote login)
rsh (remote shell)
rstat
ruptime
rwho (remote who)

trusted hosts for r-services can be found in
/etc/hosts.equiv

per-user config for trusted hosts is in 
cat .rhosts



you can login with:
rlogin <target-ip> -l <username>

and list authenticated users with login using:
rwho

+ additional details:
rusers -al <target-ip>
